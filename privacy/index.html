FamHealth Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026. App: FamHealth (Android package ai.thatched.famhealth). Publisher: Thatched.

In one sentence

FamHealth does not collect your data. Everything you put into the app stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores the family profiles, scanned or imported papers, notes, medicine names, dates and reminders that you enter. This information is kept only on your device, in an encrypted database and encrypted files. The encryption key stays in the Android Keystore of your device. We (Thatched) never receive this information and cannot see it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-up or login.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.
  • No network access: the release version of the app does not request the Internet permission and makes no network calls.
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone, because we do not have any data.
  • Text recognition (OCR) of scanned papers runs on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

Things you can choose to do

  • Camera. If you scan a paper, the app uses your camera. Pictures are processed on the device.
  • Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Lock-screen text is generic and does not show names or medicines.
  • Encrypted backup file. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this app. If you want a copy, you can create a backup file protected by a passphrase you choose. The file goes only where you put it (for example a folder or another app you pick). We cannot read it and cannot recover it if you forget the passphrase.
  • Sharing. If you share a paper or file with another app, that is your action and the other app's own privacy policy applies.

Deleting your data

Use "Delete all data" in the app settings to erase all profiles, papers, reminders and the encryption key from the device. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Backup files you created yourself must be deleted by you.

Not a medical device

FamHealth is an organizer for papers, notes and reminders. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. It gives no medical advice. Always consult a qualified health professional before making any health decision. Text read from scanned papers can contain mistakes, so check every field against the original paper.

Children

The app is meant for adults who manage health papers for their family, including children. It is not directed at children, and children do not use accounts because there are none. Because no data is sent to us, we do not collect any child's personal data.

Legal basis and your rights

Because we receive no personal data, we are not a controller or processor of the content you keep in the app (GDPR, India DPDP Act 2023 and similar laws). You control the data on your device and can view, export or erase it at any time.

Changes

If a future version ever sends data off the device, this policy will be updated first, and the app will ask for your consent before anything is sent. The date at the top shows the latest version.
